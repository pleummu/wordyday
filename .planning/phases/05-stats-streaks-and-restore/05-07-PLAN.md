**As a** player, **I want to** see accurate Win Streaks for each Board, one global Day Streak protected by up to 3 Restores a month, and full statistics for each mode and length, **so that** I can track my progress and have a reason to come back every day.

<objective>
User story slice: **As a** player who keeps the game open across days or loses a streak without choosing to, **I want** the streak rules to apply the moment the date changes and a short, friendly note when my streak ends, **so that** I always know where my streak stands.

</objective>
