## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** see accurate Win Streaks for each Board, one global Day Streak protected by up to 3 Restores a month, and full statistics for each mode and length, **so that** I can track my progress and have a reason to come back every day.

<objective>
User story slice: **As a** player who missed a day or more, **I want to** be offered a Restore that keeps my Day Streak (up to 3 a month), **so that** one missed day does not wipe out a long streak.

Purpose: the Restore state machine (SPEC section 8, E1 CHECK on open, E2 RESTORE, E3 DECLINE) with its EC table, the blocking prompt, and the boot check. The streak-ended modal, focus/midnight triggers and the finish-time CHECK (EC-39, EC-40) follow in 05-07.
Output: streaks.ts reducers + EC rows, store actions, Modal dismissible option, RestorePrompt, e2e/restore.spec.ts.
</objective>
