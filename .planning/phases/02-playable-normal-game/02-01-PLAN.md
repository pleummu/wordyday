## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** play a complete, correctly scored Normal game at 4, 5 or 6 letters in the browser, **so that** I can enjoy endless Wordle at the length I choose.

<objective>

Purpose: Scoring correctness is the project's core value. These modules have no React, clock, storage or DOM access, so the store and UI in later plans can rely on them.
