| browser -> dialog | The browser may close a dialog without a cancel event |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-12 | Elevation (anti-farming) | giveUp / forfeitNewGame | mitigate | Store re-checks `canForfeit` inside each action, not only in the UI; tests cover every other attempt count, revealing and finished states for all lengths |
| T-04-13 | Repudiation (double count) | finishGame seam | mitigate | Seam called exactly once per finish at submit/giveUp/forfeit; finishReveal never calls it; tests assert call counts |
| T-04-14 | Denial of service | Modal native close | mitigate | `onClose` on the dialog guarded by `openRef` so the store flag is cleared on browser closes and not double-fired on programmatic closes |
</threat_model>
