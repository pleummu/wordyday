## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** keep a separate saved game on each of the six Boards that survives refreshes, mode and length switches and broken storage, with a fair Give Up and a way to reset everything, **so that** I can come back to any game later without ever losing progress.

<objective>
Deliver the six-Boards slice: switching mode or length becomes immediate and non-forfeiting, each Board keeps its own game (in memory and in the saved document), the switch confirm is removed, the Wordubado popup flag is saved, and the length picker shows which other lengths have a game in progress. Turns `e2e/boards.spec.ts` green.

Purpose: SAVE-01/02 are the user-visible heart of "six Boards"; the dot (D-12) makes saved games discoverable.
Output: `switchBoard`, removal of SwitchConfirm path, LengthPicker dot, updated tests and Wordubado E2E.
</objective>
