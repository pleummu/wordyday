## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** get a satisfying result screen when a game ends, with the answer, my stats, a definition and a way to share, **so that** I can replay, learn the word and show my result to friends.

<objective>
User story slice: **As a** Wordubado player who just solved a slang word, **I want** "Show definition" to show the hand-written slang meaning instantly, **so that** I learn what the meme or Gen Z word actually means, even offline and even when the dictionary would give a different, everyday sense.

Purpose: completes RSLT-02's curated slang path and the D-08 source choice (and D-11), via the build-time word data (Claude's discretion: a defs field in each lazy words-N.json chunk).
Output: reviewedDefs + defs in serialisePools, regenerated words-N.json, check-bundle defs sentinel, Pools.defs, curated branch in DefinitionPanel.
</objective>
