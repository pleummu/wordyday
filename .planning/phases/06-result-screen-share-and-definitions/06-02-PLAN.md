## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** get a satisfying result screen when a game ends, with the answer, my stats, a definition and a way to share, **so that** I can replay, learn the word and show my result to friends.

<objective>
User story slice: **As a** player who closed the result modal or came back to a finished Board, **I want** a slim banner that says how the game ended with "See result" and "Play again", **so that** I can look at my result again or move on, without the screen getting crowded.

Output: slim Banner, retired streak/banner copy, Win Streak reset suffix in the modal, layout.spec checks; result.spec and layout.spec green.
</objective>
