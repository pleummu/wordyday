## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** get a satisfying result screen when a game ends, with the answer, my stats, a definition and a way to share, **so that** I can replay, learn the word and show my result to friends.

<objective>
User story slice: **As a** player who finished a game, **I want to** tap "Show definition" and read what the word means, **so that** I learn something from every game, and I am told plainly when no definition can be fetched.

Purpose: delivers RSLT-02's dictionaryapi.dev path and every failure path (D-07, D-09, D-10, D-24..D-26). In this plan every answer, including Wordubado slang, goes through the dictionary lookup; 06-06 adds the curated slang source and the D-08 choice in front of it.
Output: src/logic/definition.ts (+ tests), store definitionShown flag, DefinitionPanel, Show definition in ResultModal, e2e/definition.spec.ts.
</objective>
